About the Photographer

A quiet eye
for beautiful moments.

Dejeu Crew is led by a Central Valley wedding photographer and filmmaker who believes the most meaningful images are often the ones that feel the most like you — a hand held a little tighter, a laugh between portraits, the stillness just before the day begins.

Based in Modesto, California, she photographs and films weddings with warmth, patience, and a deep love for beautiful moments. Her approach is gently guided and never overworked: natural light, honest connection, and room for the day to unfold in its own way.

The result is refined, quietly luxurious wedding photography and videography that still feels true. Every couple is welcomed into a thoughtful, personal process, from the first conversation to the final gallery and film — made to be lived with for years to come.

Questions

Wedding photo & video FAQ

How do we book our wedding date?

Send an inquiry through the booking page with your package and preferred date. Once we confirm availability and the right package for your day, a deposit reserves your date. The final deposit amount is confirmed when we reply.

Do you offer both wedding photography and wedding videography?

Yes. Dejeu Crew offers wedding photography and cinematic wedding videography. Photography packages start at $2,500 for 8 hours (a minimum of 200 photos) and $3,000 for 10 hours. Videography packages start at $2,500 for 8 hours and $3,000 for 10 hours. You can book photo, video, or both.

How many photos will we receive?

The 8-hour wedding photography package includes a minimum of 200 photos. For 10-hour coverage, ask in your inquiry and we’ll walk you through what to expect for your day.

How many hours of coverage are included?

Wedding photography and videography packages are available with 8 or 10 hours of coverage. Engagement sessions are 1 hour.

Do you offer engagement sessions?

Yes. Engagement sessions start at $500 for a 1-hour couples session — a relaxed way to get comfortable in front of the camera before the wedding.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

Dejeu Crew is based in Modesto, California, and photographs and films weddings throughout the Central Valley, including Stockton, Turlock, Manteca, Merced, and the Sacramento area. Planning a wedding somewhere else? Share your venue in your inquiry and we’ll confirm the details.

Are the listed prices final?

Prices are starting rates. Your final quote is confirmed after we talk through your date, location, and coverage.
